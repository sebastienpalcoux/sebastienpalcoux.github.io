CLASSIFICATION · 2026

How far can
fusion rules take us?

Classifies integral Grothendieck rings up to rank five and treats several higher-rank cases. Egyptian fractions, Normaliz computations, and induction to the Drinfeld center combine arithmetic restrictions with exact constructions and exclusions, including examples beyond the Isaacs property.

Classifying integral Grothendieck rings up to rank 5 and beyond
Letters in Mathematical Physics 116, 141 (2026)

Paper & citation ↗
RIGIDITY · 2026

When algebra objects are locally rigid

Establishes local rigidity and effective finiteness for separable algebra objects and their homomorphisms. Exchange relations turn Frobenius subalgebra questions into idempotent geometry, with applications to coideal subalgebras, intermediate operator algebras, and quantitative bounds for intermediate subfactors.

Local Ocneanu rigidity for separable algebra objects
Preprint · arXiv:2609.10440

Paper & citation ↗
MODULAR DATA · 2026

Arithmetic meets classification

Classifies integral modular data up to rank thirteen and integral half-Frobenius fusion rings up to rank twelve. Egyptian fractions with squared denominators, arithmetic restrictions, and exact computations organize the search and constrain the next ranks, including odd-dimensional and perfect cases.

Classification of integral modular data up to rank 13
Journal of Mathematical Physics 67, 081701 (2026)
