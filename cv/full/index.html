My interests span fusion and tensor categories, operator algebras, subfactors, planar algebras, finite groups, and algebraic combinatorics.

I study the interplay between these subjects through categorification, rigidity, and quantum arithmetic. I also develop computational resources for the classification of fusion rings and co-organize the BIMSA–Tsinghua Quantum Symmetry Seminar.
