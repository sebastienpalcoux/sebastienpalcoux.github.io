Fusion categories & categorification

Fusion rings encode the possible rules for tensor products. A central challenge is to decide which of these rings can arise from fusion categories over the complex numbers. My work combines structural criteria, explicit constructions, and classification.

A FIRST EXAMPLE

One rule.
A whole sequence.

The Fibonacci fusion ring has two basis elements, 1 and τ, with the rule τ ⊗ τ = 1 ⊕ τ. Repeated tensor products bring the Fibonacci numbers into the picture.

τ⁵ = 3 · 1 + 5 · τ

Each extra tensor factor replaces the coefficient pair (a, b) by (b, a + b). Fusion rules record how a tensor product decomposes into simple objects; categorification asks for a tensor category realizing those rules.
